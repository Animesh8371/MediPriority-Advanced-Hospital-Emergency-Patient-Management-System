Appointments

Normal (non-emergency) appointment scheduling, backed by a strict FIFO queue in C in addition to MySQL persistence — unlike emergency cases, appointments are never reprioritized.

Book an appointment

Use a unix timestamp for the MVP demo (e.g. paste the output of date +%s, or any future integer). A friendlier date picker is a natural next iteration.

Call next in line

Pulls the next appointment from the front of the C FIFO queue (booked-order, not priority-order).
