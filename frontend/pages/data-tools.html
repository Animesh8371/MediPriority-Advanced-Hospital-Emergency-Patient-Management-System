Synthetic patient generator

Each generated patient goes through the real admission pipeline: registered, queued by priority, then given a bed and doctor. Watch the beds and doctors fill up.

All names, ages and phone numbers are randomly generated and fictional. Phones use the 98765xxxxx pattern from seed.sql, so a number could coincide with a real one — do not contact them.
